The shocking feature list

File management.
What a concept.

Built for people who have, at some point, tried to manage a file on a Mac.

01

A path bar you can type in.

Enter a path. Autocomplete it. Go there. Yes, this needed to be said.

02

Cut means cut.

⌘X and ⌘V move files like every reasonable person expects.

03

Transfers with receipts.

Real progress, byte counts, speed, errors, pause, resume, and cancel.

04

Your right click, promoted.

New file, new folder, Terminal here, copy path, checksums, and more.

05

Sorting for grown-ups.

Use more than one criterion. Your folders contain multitudes.

06

A tree. If you want one.

Expandable folder navigation in the sidebar, without forcing it on everyone.

07

Your server is invited.

Mount SMB shares with native macOS authentication—without taking a field trip through Finder.

08

Your whole setup, remembered.

Named workspaces restore panes, tabs, views, and sorting. Pick up exactly where you meant to.

09

Image chores, handled.

Rotate, resize, and convert copies without sending your pictures on a field trip.

10

Search that actually goes looking.

Search this folder, every folder below it, your Home folder, or the whole Mac—with content and useful filters.
